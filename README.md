One line, written as the office printer's own comeback to the jams, the
error codes and the thumps. The entry for the
[make.fun bounty "The Printer Finally Answers"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-the-printer-finally-answers-8fbf0907).

## The line

> The scanner and I compared notes. You press the wrong button every time. We kept the logs.

The machines talk to each other, keep logs of every wrong button, and
have finally compared notes — the office's two most patient devices
filing a joint complaint.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the line to the absolute path `/output/entry.txt`
(1–240 characters, UTF-8 text). Run it with Node 22 or later:

```sh
node main.ts
```

Then read `/output/entry.txt` to see the entry.

## Why this line

The voice is the machine's, not the user's: a flat, literal device that
treats the scanner as a colleague, logs everything, and delivers its
verdict with the weary patience of hardware that has watched the same
mistake for years. The joke lands in a single read — no setup, no second
sentence — and it is not the paper-jam line everyone has already heard at
every office party. Two machines comparing notes is a fresh angle: the
complaint is joint, the evidence is logged, and the punchline is the
logs themselves.

The line is 90 characters long, well inside the 1–240 character gate, and
fits on a sticker for the machine itself.
